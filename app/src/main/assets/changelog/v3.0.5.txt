SUPERTUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 3.0.5
DATE: 2026-11-10
STATUS: release

HIGHLIGHTS
- TV Shows in Movies section: integrated full TV show catalog with season & episode browsing, episode switching drawer, and automatic resume memory
- Shorts tap-to-refresh: re-tapping the Shorts navigation bar icon smoothly scrolls back to the top short or refreshes the feed
- Silent Shorts refresh: seamless background retry with exponential backoff on feed refresh, completely eliminating error dialogs and snackbars
- Shorts flush right-edge layout: repositioned action buttons flush to the far right bezel with ergonomic semi-transparent buttons and expanded video canvas
- Zero background battery drain: strict sleeping lifecycle for TV and movie network calls, waking only when entering the section

SHORTS ENHANCEMENTS
- Bottom bar navigation reselect: tapping the Shorts icon while scrolled down returns to reel #0; tapping at the top fetches fresh recommendations
- Resilient background retry: 3-stage silent retry loop (1s, 2s, 4s) ensuring interrupted network connections never discard your loaded queue
- Error dialog suppression: feed refresh errors are caught silently, preserving current playback and keeping modal error dialogs away
- Right-bezel aligned controls: reduced edge padding to 0dp (with 2dp glass curvature margin), freeing up 80%+ of central screen space for vertical video
- Thumb-friendly action buttons: clean PlayerScrimAffordance semi-transparent circular buttons for effortless one-handed thumb interaction

MOVIES & TV SHOWS
- TV show catalog integration: browse trending, popular, and top-rated TV shows alongside movies
- Seasons & episode selector: easily switch between seasons and episodes with detailed metadata and episode thumbnails
- Seamless episode resumption: remembers the exact second and episode watched across all TV shows and movies
- Complete sleeping lifecycle: zero network activity until section is entered, instantly cancelling pending requests on departure
- Unified 7-layer ad shield: full popup ad blocker and sandbox engine protecting all TV streaming servers
